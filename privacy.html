Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MYTHIC CONCLAVE handles information in the current iOS/iPadOS version of the game (bundle identifier com.magiccreation.mythicconclave).

1. Data stored on your device

The game stores gameplay information locally on your device, including your hero collection, virtual Coin balance, settings, enhancement progress and match totals. These local records are not uploaded to a developer-operated server.

2. No developer account, advertising or tracking

MYTHIC CONCLAVE does not require a developer-operated user account. The current version does not include third-party advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs or cross-app tracking. The developer does not sell personal information.

3. Game Center

Online multiplayer may use Apple Game Center for player authentication, matchmaking and real-time multiplayer connectivity. Game Center is an Apple service and information processed by Game Center is handled under Apple's own terms and privacy practices. MYTHIC CONCLAVE does not operate a separate matchmaking account database.

4. Nearby multiplayer and Local Network permission

If you choose nearby multiplayer, the app uses Apple's local networking technologies to discover and connect to a nearby compatible device. During a match, game-related data such as roster information, card levels, battle state and commands may be exchanged with the other player's device. A game player label is used rather than intentionally transmitting your device's personal name.

5. In-App Purchases

Optional virtual Coin purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. The developer does not receive your payment card or banking information. The app receives purchase-related status needed to deliver the purchased in-game item.

6. Backup and restore

If you export a local backup, the backup may contain your local collection, progress and settings. You choose where that file is saved or shared using Apple's system share interface. Importing a backup can replace existing local progress after confirmation.

7. Support communications

If you contact support by email, the information you choose to include in that email is used to respond to your request and troubleshoot the issue. Do not send payment card details, passwords or other unnecessary sensitive information.

8. Data deletion

Because gameplay progress is stored locally, deleting the app may remove local progress unless you have created and retained a compatible backup. Information held by Apple services such as Game Center or App Store purchase history is controlled through your Apple account and Apple's services.

9. Children

The game is not designed to collect personal information directly from children through a developer-operated account or server. Apple services and device-level family or purchase controls may apply independently.

10. Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change in a future release, this policy may be updated to reflect those changes. The updated date shown above will be revised when appropriate.

11. Contact

For privacy or support questions, email t3100550@gmail.com.

This policy describes the current MYTHIC CONCLAVE app architecture: local game progress, Apple Game Center, Apple StoreKit and optional Local Network multiplayer.
